# Competitive Separation

The public experience must not look interchangeable with a generic motherhood, wellness, homestead, minimalist-lifestyle, or AI-generated blog template.

## Separation gate

Before approval, confirm that the design communicates all three of these traits without relying only on the logo:

- Family field journal: authored, editorial, and experience-aware.
- Practical household capability: readers can learn and do something concrete.
- Intentional simplicity: calm and selective, not sparse for fashion's sake.

Reject solutions dominated by interchangeable hero slogans, stock-family imagery, uniform icon cards, vague wellness claims, ornamental botanical clichés, or samurai imagery.

Do not copy Kids Color Station's print-shop framing, crayon palette, children's activity motifs, or library interface patterns. Shared technical QA skills may be reused; product identity may not.
