# Visual Language

## Direction

Build a warm editorial system with tactile restraint. Favor clear hierarchy, generous breathing room, readable line length, fine rules, and authentic household photography.

Use these colors as a direction, then verify every final pairing for accessible contrast:

- Warm paper: `#F5EFE4`
- Soft canvas: `#FCFAF6`
- Deep ink: `#24312D`
- Forest: `#315744`
- Muted olive: `#788063`
- Clay: `#A45F45`
- Restrained gold: `#C49A45`

Use olive and gold primarily for accents unless the tested combination meets the required contrast. Never use decorative gold as body text.

Prefer Fraunces for selected editorial display moments and Source Sans 3 for reading, using the repository's established font-loading approach when technically appropriate. Preserve fallbacks and avoid layout shift.

## Recognizable motifs

- Chapter labels
- Field-note details
- Fine divider rules
- Small margin annotations
- Practical checklists
- Authentic household photography

Use motifs selectively. Do not decorate every surface or force all content into matching cards.

## Avoid

- Emoji as interface graphics
- Generic gradients and rainbow calls to action
- Glassmorphism
- Excessive rounded cards or pills
- Faux-handwritten clutter
- Generic beige “crunchy mom” styling
- Warrior or samurai clichés
- Decorative texture that hurts reading clarity
