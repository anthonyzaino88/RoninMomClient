# Voice and Copy

Write as experienced but not preachy, warm but not sentimental, confident but transparent, and plainspoken rather than promotional.

## Copy pattern

1. Name the real reader situation.
2. Briefly establish relevant personal context when it is true.
3. Move quickly into organized, practical value.
4. State limitations, tradeoffs, or uncertainty where they matter.
5. End with a useful next step rather than a generic inspirational flourish.

Prefer concrete nouns and verbs. Replace broad phrases such as “embrace holistic wellness” with the specific practice or outcome the page actually covers.

Never invent a family story, quote, test result, credential, outcome, date, or claim. Do not expose private family information merely to make copy feel personal.
