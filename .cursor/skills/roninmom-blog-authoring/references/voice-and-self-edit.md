# Voice and Self-Edit

Ronin Mom should sound like a thoughtful parent sharing what has genuinely helped while respecting the reader's ability to decide. The voice is warm, candid, capable, observant, and practical. It is not preachy, performatively perfect, clinical, or overly polished.

## Voice choices

- Use first person for confirmed experience and second person sparingly for useful guidance.
- Admit tradeoffs, uncertainty, and imperfect days where truthful.
- Prefer “what worked for us” over universal claims.
- Use ordinary language before specialist terms; define a term when it materially helps.
- Let warmth come from specificity and care, not pet names, exclamation points, or canned encouragement.
- Keep natural or low-toxin preferences grounded in function and evidence, not fear.

## Generic-AI warning signs

Rewrite passages that rely on:

- “In today's fast-paced world” or other universal scene-setting
- “Whether you're a seasoned…or just starting out”
- “It's important to remember,” “game-changer,” “journey,” or “unlock” without specific meaning
- Repeated three-item lists and symmetrical paragraph structures
- Excessive em dashes, rhetorical questions, or one-sentence paragraphs
- Headings that repeat the title without advancing the answer
- Empty transitions such as “Now that we've explored…”
- Unsupported superlatives, neat moral lessons, or a relentlessly upbeat tone
- A conclusion that paraphrases the entire article instead of helping the reader act

## Self-edit passes

1. **Reader value:** Highlight the exact sentence, step, or decision aid that earns each section's place. Cut sections that lack one.
2. **Truth:** Label each personal, factual, and causal claim. Confirm support or revise the framing.
3. **Structure:** Move essential advice earlier. Make headings answerable and distinct.
4. **Specificity:** Replace abstractions with examples, constraints, amounts, or tradeoffs when confirmed.
5. **Voice:** Read aloud. Break mechanical rhythm, remove filler, and restore natural phrasing.
6. **Trust:** Remove diagnosis, certainty, shame, toxin fear, and implied expertise. Add limitations or professional guidance when warranted.
7. **Finish:** Verify the title, introduction, article body, and closing all promise and deliver the same outcome.
