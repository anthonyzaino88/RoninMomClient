# Ronin Mom Blog Authoring

Read [Article shapes](references/article-shapes.md) to choose a structure and [Voice and self-edit](references/voice-and-self-edit.md) before drafting. Also read `../roninmom-brand-direction/references/voice-and-copy.md` and the trust guidance in `../roninmom-seo-audit/references/trust-and-ymyl.md` when the subject is health, children, food, remedies, cleaning, safety, money, or another trust-sensitive area.

## Truth boundary

Before writing, separate:

- Confirmed family experience the author has supplied
- Facts that require current authoritative support
- Practical advice that can be framed as options or judgment
- Unknown details that must be asked, omitted, or marked for verification

Never manufacture a personal anecdote, result, quote, product test, credential, statistic, source, or child detail. If research access is unavailable, leave a clear verification note instead of inventing support.

## Workflow

1. Write a brief stating the reader, exact question, useful outcome, primary pillar, search intent, distinct Ronin Mom angle, and claims requiring evidence.
2. Research material claims with current primary or authoritative sources. Record the source, supported claim, publication or update date when available, and access date. Keep inference separate from sourced fact.
3. Choose an article shape. Build an outline that solves the reader's problem in the order they will encounter it.
4. Draft a title and opening that are specific and inviting without clickbait. Confirm the outcome early.
5. Add personal context only when confirmed and useful. Aim for a practical guide supported by experience, not a diary entry padded into advice.
6. Use concrete steps, examples, tradeoffs, limitations, and decision help. Anticipate the next question rather than repeating the premise.
7. Add natural internal-link opportunities and a reader-first closing action. Do not force keywords or newsletter promotion.
8. Run the self-edit in the voice reference. Then send the complete draft to the read-only `blog-editor`; revise every blocking issue before publishing.
9. Hand the approved draft to `roninmom-content-feature` for MDX, frontmatter, routes, assets, and final implementation.

## Draft standard

- Answer one main question fully enough that the reader can act.
- Use a genuine opening: a recognizable problem, observation, or confirmed moment—not throat-clearing.
- Prefer precise nouns and verbs, varied sentence length, and useful headings.
- Explain why a recommendation fits, when it may not, and what a reasonable alternative is.
- Distinguish lived experience, opinion, and sourced guidance in the prose.
- Protect family privacy and avoid making the children the product.
- Cut repetition, inflated claims, generic encouragement, and conclusions that only restate the introduction.

Do not assign a word count before the outline. Let the reader question determine length.
