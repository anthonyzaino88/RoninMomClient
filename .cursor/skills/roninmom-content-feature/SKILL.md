# Ronin Mom Content Feature

Read the references relevant to the task:

- Always read [Content model](references/content-model.md) before changing content files, frontmatter, loaders, or article routes.
- Always read [Editorial quality](references/editorial-quality.md) before writing or materially revising public content.
- Read [Legacy content migration](references/legacy-content-migration.md) before migrating, featuring, redirecting, or materially revising an existing article.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the actual content schema, loaders, route generation, and representative published posts.
2. Preserve published slugs and URLs unless the task explicitly includes redirects.
3. Confirm the approved draft, primary pillar, search intent, and reader outcome before encoding it.
4. Preserve the draft's intended hierarchy with semantic MDX and existing editorial components.
5. Add internal links where they genuinely help the reader and related-content data where the schema supports it.
6. For legacy content, assign a keep, revise, hold, or retire recommendation before implementation; require user approval before deletion or unpublishing.
7. Validate frontmatter, generated routes, and the production build after content-model changes.

Avoid keyword stuffing, fabricated expertise or testing, invented quotes, unsupported health or product-safety claims, fear-based toxin language, and padded prose.
