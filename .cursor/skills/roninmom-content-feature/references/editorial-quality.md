# Editorial Quality

## Publishing quality gate

Use `roninmom-blog-authoring` to create or substantively revise the draft. Before publishing, confirm that the approved draft still meets these standards after MDX and component implementation.

- Serve one clear reader question.
- Connect naturally to one primary content pillar.
- Open with a specific problem, observation, or truthful family experience.
- Move quickly into practical value.
- Use descriptive headings, concrete steps, limitations, and a useful conclusion.
- Link to related Ronin Mom content when it advances the task.
- Write meaningful image alt text that describes purpose and visible content; use empty alt for purely decorative images.

## Trust and sourcing

Clearly distinguish personal experience from established guidance. For health, food, natural-remedy, child, low-toxin, cleaning, disinfecting, and safety subjects:

- Do not diagnose, promise outcomes, or frame preference as medical fact.
- Use reliable sources for material factual claims.
- Name uncertainty and situations that warrant qualified professional help.
- Do not imply credentials, product testing, or firsthand results that the author has not confirmed.
- Do not call a homemade mixture a disinfectant or claim it kills germs without evidence supporting the exact formulation, use, surface, and contact time.
- Prefer precise terms such as clean, remove residue, or reduce waste when those are the outcomes the evidence supports.

Protect family privacy. Personal detail must earn its place by helping the reader.
