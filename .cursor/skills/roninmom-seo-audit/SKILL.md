# Ronin Mom SEO Audit

Read both direct references:

- [SEO checklist](references/seo-checklist.md)
- [Trust-sensitive content](references/trust-and-ymyl.md)

## Workflow

1. Identify the route's reader intent and visible purpose.
2. Inspect source, then verify rendered HTML or exported output.
3. Trace canonical, metadata base, indexability, sitemap, robots, headings, internal links, and structured data.
4. Check social previews, images, performance risks, and unnecessary client JavaScript.
5. Report findings in severity order.

For every finding include: confirmed or optional, evidence, route/file, reader/search impact, and a specific fix. Do not emit schema that visible content does not support.
