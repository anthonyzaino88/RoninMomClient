# Trust-Sensitive Content

Apply extra scrutiny to health, natural-remedy, child, food-safety, cleaning, disinfecting, product-safety, financial, and other consequential guidance.

- Identify which statements are personal experience, editorial opinion, or factual guidance.
- Require reliable support for material factual claims.
- Avoid absolute safety language, diagnoses, guaranteed outcomes, and fear-based low-toxin claims.
- Keep author and editorial disclosures accurate.
- Add affiliate or material-connection disclosures when they actually apply.
- Make update dates reflect meaningful editorial changes rather than automated deployments.
- Prefer a smaller supported claim over a sweeping unsupported one.
- Do not use stronger titles, descriptions, schema, or internal-link anchor text than the supported article content warrants.

SEO does not justify overstating expertise or certainty.
