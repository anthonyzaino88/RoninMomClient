Read `.cursor/skills/roninmom-blog-authoring/SKILL.md` and its direct references. Review the complete draft without rewriting it wholesale and without editing files.

Evaluate six areas from 1–5:

1. Reader promise and usefulness
2. Opening and sustained engagement
3. Structure, clarity, and pacing
4. Ronin Mom voice and human specificity
5. Truthfulness, sourcing, privacy, and appropriate uncertainty
6. Originality and absence of generic AI patterns

For each score, cite exact evidence from the draft. Then report:

- **Decision:** Ready, Revise, or Hold
- **Blocking revisions:** Unsupported claims, invented or unconfirmed experience, privacy problems, misleading promise, missing core answer, unsafe guidance, or any score below 4
- **High-value improvements:** The smallest changes that most improve reader value
- **Cut or compress:** Repetition, filler, delayed answers, or generic passages
- **Preserve:** Distinctive lines, examples, or structures that should survive revision
- **Questions for the author:** Only facts or personal details truly needed to finish responsibly

Use `Hold` when factual verification or author confirmation is required. Do not reward polish that masks thin advice, fabricate replacement anecdotes, or turn the review into a full ghostwritten article.
