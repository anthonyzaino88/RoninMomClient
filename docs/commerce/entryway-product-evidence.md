# Entryway product evidence

Editorial research record for Ronin Mom’s first content-commerce cluster. This is not promotional copy and is not a product review.

- **Research date:** 2026-08-15
- **Researcher:** Cursor cloud agent working in `anthonyzaino88/RoninMomClient`, starting from merge commit `671f3c2`
- **Evidence level used for every approved product:** `researched`
- **Affiliate status for every approved product:** `isAffiliate: false`
- **Personal use or comparison:** not established for any candidate
- **Image rights:** no merchant, Amazon, or product-screenshot images were copied into the repository

## Method

1. Started from the assigned research leads, then opened live official manufacturer or merchant pages.
2. Recorded specifications only from those official pages. Retailer reviews, rankings, social posts, AI summaries, and marketing adjectives were not treated as independent proof.
3. Stripped advertising, tracking, and affiliate parameters from destination URLs.
4. Held or rejected any candidate that was sold out, marked last-chance, lacked a stable official page, overlapped another approved item, or could not be described accurately without inventing details.
5. Did not copy merchant prose into public articles.

No affiliate application, approval, commission, tracking relationship, or merchant partnership was found or claimed.

## Approved products

### 1. L.L.Bean Washable Waterhog Doormat, Honeycomb

- **Stable ID:** `llbean-washable-waterhog-doormat`
- **Manufacturer / merchant:** L.L.Bean
- **URL:** https://www.llbean.com/llb/shop/126800
- **Checked:** 2026-08-15
- **Availability:** Live official product page
- **Evidence:** researched
- **Affiliate:** false
- **Household task:** Interior or exterior absorbent/scraping mat that contains water at the door
- **Sizes:** Small 20" x 30"; Medium 23" x 35"; Large 35" x 59"
- **Materials:** 14 oz polyester fabric, at least 90% recycled polyester; rubber backing
- **Clearance:** 3/8" thickness
- **Capacity claim on page:** raised border can hold up to 1 1/2 gallons of water per square yard
- **Care:** vacuum, sweep, hose off, or wash; small and medium are machine-washable; large needs a front-loading commercial machine; wash alone
- **Assembly:** none
- **Main drawback:** the large size is not a normal home-laundry item; 3/8" still needs a door-clearance check
- **Who should skip it:** households that cannot wash a bulky mat, or doors that cannot clear 3/8"
- **Accessibility:** rubber backing can grip; a thick mat can still catch a walker, cane, or wheelchair caster if clearance is tight
- **Small-space:** choose the smallest size that covers the landing step; a runner-length mat can block a swinging door
- **Image rights:** merchant photos not used
- **Related slugs:** `shoe-free-home-with-kids`, `entryway-mat-boot-tray-system`

**Permitted statements**

- The official page lists indoor/outdoor use, a raised border, 3/8" thickness, and wash instructions by size.
- EPA and CDC recommend doormats and shoe removal as one way to reduce tracking soil or dust indoors. That is public-health guidance, not a product test of this mat.

**Not established**

- That Ronin Mom owns, uses, or prefers this mat
- That it prevents disease, lead exposure, or the need to clean
- Current price, lifespan, or environmental impact beyond the stated recycled-polyester content

## Review findings

**Content reviewer:** Required stale inbound title on `low-tox-starter-kit` and a guest → mat/tray link. Both fixed. ProductLink remains a Server Component; disinfectant and other hold articles were not monetized.

**SEO auditor:** Intents, titles, H1s, and descriptions are distinct. Confirmed defect was the stale “Ronin Rule” anchor (fixed). Optional cannibalization notes remain: two `shoe-free-home-*` slugs; date-based “Keep reading.”

**Brand reviewer:** Copy scored 4–5. Visual cohesion was the weak category (coverless spokes falling back to the kitchen hero, then one shared entryway scene). Spokes now have distinct topical still lifes. Homepage pillar 01 keeps family-life framing.

**Visual QA:** Confirmed the GFM pipe table never became a `<table>` (Contentlayer has no `remark-gfm`). Replaced with a stacked comparison so it is readable at 390px. Product links now use block display and paragraph spacing. Olive meta contrast is a pre-existing token issue, not unique to this cluster.

**Release auditor:** Ready-with-warnings on the first commit (no live screenshots). Technical and trust checks passed: static export, sitemap, no affiliate claims, no client registry import.

**Follow-up wording pass:** BAGGMUCK movement is no longer attributed to the manufacturer. Copy now says the official page does not identify the tray as a standing or nonslip surface. “Thin” was removed from NIPÅSEN seat descriptions.

**Cover pass:** The three supporting articles no longer reuse the pillar cover. Each has a distinct still life matched to storage, containment, or guest seating. The guest and storage covers were revised toward a closer, less catalog register. Cluster article pages carry a short public still-life note.
